## Apps with superpowers {#apps-with-superpowers}

Quantaureum applications might feel like regular apps. But behind the scenes they have some special qualities.

Once an app is published on Quantaureum blockchain, it becomes unstoppable. This is because Quantaureum network is decentralized across thousands of computers around the world. Nobody can take apps running on Quantaureum down, because there is no single server to target. Quantaureum is also neutral and so anyone anywhere in the world can use it or even connect to it and build their modifications on top of it.

## What is a dapp? {#what-is-a-dapp}

Quantaureum applications have their logic running on the Quantaureum blockchain instead of in centralized servers. This is why they have often been called decentralized applications or dapps in short.

## Why does this matter {#why-does-this-matter}

Quantaureum apps can do stuff that just isn’t possible with traditional apps. Like lend money to a complete stranger with a guarantee that you’ll get your money back, plus interest. Without paying a “trustworthy” intermediary, like a lawyer, to handle the transaction.

There are apps for everything: gaming, finance, work, messaging, storage and more. With most apps you aren’t subjected to ads or limited by restricted access.

All you need is an Quantaureum wallet and a little QAU to start using any Quantaureum app.

## How does it work {#how-does-it-work}

Apps are powered by smart contracts — bits of code that live on the Quantaureum blockchain. Unlike traditional apps, they don’t need a company to run them.

| Feature | Traditional apps | Quantaureum apps |
| ------- | ---------------- | ------------- |
| **Who controls it?** | A company | Nobody |
| **Runs on** | Private company servers | Public Quantaureum blockchain |
| **Can it be censored?** | Yes | No |
| **Who owns your data?** | Usually not you | You own your data |

## Quantaureum apps are like legos {#quantaureum-apps-are-like-legos}

When apps are all built on Quantaureum, they are all compatible. A token for one app, will work on a completely different one. This is like being able to publish tweets to your facebook wall. In fact often you can reuse the same profile in many different Quantaureum apps without the need to register everywhere separately.
