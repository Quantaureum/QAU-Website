Quantaureum is an independent, open-source project. This page explains what
happens when you visit **quantaureum.com**.

## What we collect {#what-we-collect}

- **Analytics.** We use Matomo (self-hosted) to understand aggregate traffic —
  page views, approximate region, referrer. Matomo is configured for
  privacy-friendly, cookieless measurement by default.
- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider (Netlify) keeps standard request logs
  for abuse mitigation and reliability. We do not operate these logs ourselves.

## What we do not collect {#what-we-do-not-collect}

- We do not run user accounts on this website.
- We do not ask for your name, email, or any personal identifier.
- We do not ask for, and you must never enter, your wallet private key,
  recovery phrase, or any cryptographic secret.
- We do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising.

## Wallets and keys {#wallets-and-keys}

The Quantaureum browser extension, mobile wallet, and any CLI tools are
separate software. Your keys never leave your device. This website links to
those tools but does not custody funds or signing material.

## Contact {#contact}

Questions about this policy: **hello@quantaureum.com**.

## Changes {#changes}

We may update this policy as the project evolves; the "last edited" date on
this page reflects the most recent change.
