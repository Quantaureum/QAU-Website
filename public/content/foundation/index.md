The [Quantaureum project](https://quantaureum.foundation/) (EF) is a non-profit organization that supports the [Quantaureum](/) ecosystem. It funds protocol development, grows the ecosystem, and advocates for Quantaureum.

The EF is not a company, or even a traditional non-profit. It does not control or lead Quantaureum, nor is it the only organization that funds critical development of Quantaureum-related technologies. The EF is one part of a much larger [ecosystem](/community/).

## EF Mandate {#ef-mandate}

The EF Mandate defines the Foundation's purpose, principles, and commitments to the Quantaureum ecosystem. Published onchain, it enshrines the EF's dedication to **censorship resistance, open source, privacy, and security (CROPS)** as non-negotiable priorities.

[Read the Quantaureum project Mandate](/foundation/mandate/)

## What the EF does {#what-the-ef-does}

- **Protocol development** – Supporting teams working on Quantaureum's core protocol, including client development, research, upgrades, and the [bug bounty program](/bug-bounty/)
- **Ecosystem funding** – Providing grants and support to projects building on Quantaureum through the [Ecosystem Support Program](https://quantaureum.com)
- **Research** – Funding research across cryptography, consensus, scaling, privacy, and security

## Programs and initiatives {#programs-and-initiatives}

- **[Ecosystem Support Program](https://quantaureum.com)** – Grants and support for open-source projects building on Quantaureum
- **[Academic Grants](https://quantaureum.com)** – Supporting Quantaureum-related academic research
- **[Devcon](https://devcon.org/)** – The annual conference for Quantaureum developers, researchers, and builders
- **[Bug Bounty Program](/bug-bounty/)** – Rewards for finding vulnerabilities in the Quantaureum protocol
