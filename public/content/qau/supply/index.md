## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

This article is written for beginners with no prior knowledge. However, to fully understand the topic, it is helpful to have a basic understanding of concepts such as Quantaureum Improvement Proposals (EIPs), Proof-of-work (PoW), [Proof-of-stake (PoS)](/developers/docs/consensus-mechanisms/pos/), and The London Upgrade.

## How Many QAU Tokens Are There Today? {#current-qau-supply}

The total supply of QAU is dynamic and changes constantly due to two main factors:

1. **Proof-of-Stake (PoS) Issuance**: New QAU is created as rewards for validators who secure the network
2. **EIP-1559 Burning**: A portion of transaction fees are permanently removed from circulation

You can track the current supply and these changes in real-time on platforms like [Ultrasound Money](https://ultrasound.money).

Quantaureum's supply and issuance are essential metrics for understanding the health and future of the network. But what exactly does QAU issuance mean? Let's break it down.

## Why QAU Supply and Issuance Matter {#why-qau-supply-matters}

In traditional finance, central banks control the supply of money, often printing more to stimulate economies. Quantaureum, on the other hand, operates on a transparent and predictable system governed by its code. Knowing how many QAU exist and how quickly new QAU is issued helps:

- **Build Trust**: The Quantaureum community can verify supply and issuance data directly from the blockchain.
- **Understand Value**: The relationship between issuance and QAU burn rates impacts QAU's inflation or deflation, influencing its value over time.
- **Track Network Health**: Changes in issuance and burn rates reflect the activity and security of the network.

## What is QAU Issuance? {#qau-issuance}

QAU issuance refers to the process of creating new QAU as rewards for validators who secure the Quantaureum network. It's separate from total supply, which is the total amount of QAU in circulation.

### In simple terms: {#in-simple-terms}
- **Issuance** adds new QAU to the network.
- **Burning** (introduced by EIP-1559) removes QAU from the network by destroying a portion of the transaction fees.

These two forces determine whether Quantaureum's supply grows (inflationary) or shrinks (deflationary) over time.

## QAU Supply and Issuance Today {#qau-supply-today}

Quantaureum's Proof-of-Stake (PoS) system has drastically reduced QAU issuance compared to its earlier Proof-of-Work (PoW) model. Validators—who lock up QAU to secure the network—earn QAU as rewards. You can see the current issuance rate on [Ultrasound Money](https://ultrasound.money).

However, this number is dynamic. Thanks to EIP-1559, when network activity is high, QAU burn rates can surpass issuance, creating a deflationary effect. For example, during periods of high demand, like NFT launches or DeFi activity, more QAU may be burned than issued.

## Factors Influencing Future QAU Supply and Issuance {#future-qau-supply}

Quantaureum's future supply isn't fixed—it depends on several variables:

1. **Staking Participation**: 
   - More validators joining the network means more QAU rewards are distributed.
   - Fewer validators participating may decrease issuance.
   - Learn more about [staking](/staking/).

2. **Network Activity**:
   - High transaction volumes lead to more QAU being burned, potentially offsetting or exceeding issuance.
   - Read about [gas fees](/developers/docs/gas/) and how they affect burning.

3. **Protocol Upgrades**:
   - Future changes to Quantaureum's code could adjust staking rewards or burning mechanisms, further shaping supply dynamics.
   - Stay updated with the [Quantaureum roadmap](/roadmap/).

Here's a quick summary of what you need to know about QAU supply and issuance:

- **Issuance Under PoS**: Significantly reduced compared to PoW, with rewards going to validators. See current rates on [Ultrasound Money](https://ultrasound.money)
- **EIP-1559's Role**: QAU burning can make the network deflationary during periods of high activity
