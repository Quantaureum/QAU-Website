quantaureum.com aims to use as few cookies as possible.

## Analytics: Matomo {#analytics-matomo}

We run a self-hosted **Matomo** instance to measure aggregate, anonymized
traffic. By default Matomo is configured to work without setting identifying
cookies; you can opt out below.

| Cookie  | Name    | Purpose                                            |
| ------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Matomo  | \_pk_id | Distinguishes visitors (only if cookies are allowed). |
| Matomo  | \_pk_ses | Short-lived session store (only if cookies are allowed). |

## Functional cookies {#functional-cookies}

Some site features (for example remembering your language choice) rely on a
functional cookie. These are first-party and do not track you across other
sites.

## Opt out {#opt-out}

Use the Matomo opt-out control below to disable analytics tracking for this
browser.

<MatomoOptOut />

## Blocking cookies {#blocking-cookies}

You can block all cookies in your browser settings. The site remains usable;
some preferences may not persist between visits.

## No third-party tracking {#no-third-party-tracking}

We do not embed third-party advertising, advertising cookies, or social
tracking pixels.
